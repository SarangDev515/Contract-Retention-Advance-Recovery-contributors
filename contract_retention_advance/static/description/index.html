Contract Retention & Advance Recovery

Odoo 19 · Accounting. Hold back retention, recover customer advances, and keep every step in clean journal entries.

What it does

  • Customer advance: register a customer payment as an advance liability and recover it on each progress invoice.
  • Retention: hold a percentage of each invoice and release it later, fully or partially.
  • Clean receivables: invoice gross and VAT stay unchanged; deductions are separate, reconciled entries.
  • Report: retention and advance balances with Excel export.

Use retention only, advance recovery only, or both together.

Example

Work SAR 100,000 + 15% VAT, retention 10%, advance recovery 20%.

Gross invoice115,000
Retention held (10%)10,000
Advance recovered (20%)20,000
Customer pays now85,000

How it works

1. Configure

Accounting → Configuration → Settings → Contract Retention and Advances. Enable it, set default percentages, and map a retention account, an advance account and a transfer journal.

2. Receive the customer advance

Customers → Payments → New. Record the payment (example: SAR 200,000) and confirm it.

3. Register the advance liability

On the payment, click Register Advance Liability. The amount moves from receivable to a dedicated advance liability. One advance per payment.

Dr Customer Receivable   200,000
Cr Advance Liability     200,000

4. Create the progress invoice

Customers → Invoices → New → Retention & Advance Recovery. Add the work, enable retention, select the advance and set the recovery. The Calculation Summary shows the result before posting.

5. Post the invoice

Odoo posts the normal invoice, then the module creates separate transfers and reconciles them.

Dr Retention Asset     10,000 / Cr AR 10,000
Dr Advance Liability   20,000 / Cr AR 20,000

6. Collect the payable

Use the standard Pay button for the 85,000 receipt.

7. Release retention

Reporting → Contract Retention → Release Retention. Release is manual and can be partial (example: 5,000 of 10,000). The released amount becomes a normal receivable; collect it with standard payment tools.

Dr Customer Receivable   5,000
Cr Retention Asset       5,000

8. Report and export

Reporting → Contract Retention & Advance Report. Choose date, customer and currency, click Compute, then Export Excel.

Corrections

  • Credit notes: create a linked credit note from the original invoice, before or after a release. Retention and advance recovery are corrected proportionally.
  • Reset to draft: allowed when there is no payment, release or linked credit note. History is kept; corrections are new offsetting entries.

Notes

  • Requires Odoo 19 Accounting.
  • Back up your database and test on a copy first.
  • Advance VAT treatment should be reviewed by your accountant. Not a tax, e-invoicing or ZATCA certification.
