Contract Retention & Advance Recovery

Manage customer advances, recover them across progress invoices, track contractual retention, and release retained amounts when they become collectible.

Odoo 19 Accounting Sales

Keep contract balances clear and traceable

Contract work can involve money received before invoicing, deductions held as retention, and later settlement. This module keeps these movements distinct so your team can follow the balance, review the accounting impact, and trace activity back to its source records.

01 · Advance management

Register and recover advances

Assign eligible customer payments as contract advances and apply a fixed amount or percentage to progress invoices.

02 · Retention tracking

Track amounts held back

Monitor contractual retention and release eligible amounts to customer receivables when they become collectible.

03 · Bank settlement

Reconcile receipt evidence

Use the configured outstanding receipts account and match eligible posted entries to bank receipt records.

04 · Audit & reporting

Review every movement

Use filters, source-record drill-downs, and export options to review balances and maintain an audit trail.

Three balances. Three different questions.

The module separates contract accounting balances from bank settlement status.

Balance What it tells you What changes it
Remaining Advance Prepaid money still available for future progress invoices. Posted advance recovery and corrections.
Remaining Retention Contractual retention currently held back. Posted retention releases and corrections.
Outstanding Receipts Incoming customer payments awaiting matching with bank receipt evidence. Bank reconciliation.

Advance recovery workflow

Follow the process from customer payment through invoice recovery.

1 Confirm the customer payment

Create and post the incoming customer payment, with the receivable credit available for assignment.
2 Assign and register the advance

Use Assign Contract Advance to register the advance liability. The registered assignment is immutable.
3 Apply the advance to a progress invoice

Select a registered advance and specify the recovery percentage or fixed amount.
4 Post and review the remaining balance

Posting transfers the recovered amount out of the advance liability and reduces the remaining available advance.

Example · Advance balance

Initial advance: 6,000 SAR   |   Recovered: 2,000 SAR   |   Remaining: 4,000 SAR

Recovery is limited to the available advance and eligible untaxed work.

Invoice calculation example

Illustrative values: untaxed work 10,000 SAR · tax 1,500 SAR · retention 10% · advance recovery 20% · gross invoice 11,500 SAR.

Retention release lifecycle

Retention moves from an amount held as an asset to a collectible customer receivable, then to cash when the customer pays and the receipt is reconciled.

Held
Retention asset		 → Released
Customer receivable		 → Collected
Receive & reconcile

Posting a release debits Accounts Receivable and credits the Retention account. The release makes the retained amount collectible; it does not create bank cash directly.

Configuration checklist

Configure the module under Accounting Settings → Contract Retention and Advances. Settings are company-specific.

Contract transfer accounts

  • Enable contract accounting.
  • Set a reconcilable current-asset account for retention.
  • Set a reconcilable current-liability account for advances.
  • Choose a miscellaneous journal for contract transfers.

Bank settlement accounts

  • Configure the payment journal's actual Bank account.
  • Set a separate reconcilable Outstanding Receipts account.
  • Verify the company, customer, currency, and project.

Bank reconciliation process

Use the reconciliation flow to connect the posted outstanding receipt to the actual bank receipt.

ACreate bank receipt
Confirm the actual receipt, date, journal, and reference to create the draft receipt.
BReview and post
Validate the receipt entries and return to the Reconcile Payment wizard.
CMatch the credit
Select the eligible posted Outstanding Receipts credit and confirm the full match.
Reconciliation constraints: company-currency payments only; equal full residual amounts; matching customer, company, account, and currency; and a posted two-line bank receipt are required.

Reporting, audit trail & controls

Support day-to-day review with filtered reports and traceable source records.

Reporting capabilities

  • Dynamic date, company, customer, and project filters.
  • Drill-downs into invoices, advances, and movements.
  • Print and PDF export with A4 landscape support.
  • Multi-sheet Excel export with Summary and Source sheets.

Control & audit trail

  • Registered advances and movement records are immutable.
  • Duplicate detection during receipt creation.
  • Reconciliation revalidated under standard Odoo locks.
  • Audit trail links corrections to journal entries.
