01 / THE BIG PICTURE

Three balances. Three different questions.

This module separates contract accounting from cash settlement. Do not use one balance as proof of another.

BalanceWhat it tells youWhat reduces it
Remaining advanceHow much prepaid money is still available for future invoices.Posted advance recovery and its corrections.
Remaining retentionHow much contractual retention is still held.Posted release or retention corrections.
Outstanding ReceiptsWhether the incoming payment has been matched to bank receipt evidence.Bank reconciliation—not advance allocation.
02 / SETUP

Configure the accounts before posting.

In Accounting settings, open Contract Retention and Advances. Settings are company-specific.

Contract transfer accounts

  • Enable contract accounting.
  • Configure a reconcilable current-asset account for retention.
  • Configure a reconcilable current-liability account for advances.
  • Select a miscellaneous journal for contract transfers.
  • Set default retention/recovery percentages and the handover-date policy.

Bank settlement accounts

  • Configure the payment journal’s actual Bank account.
  • Use a separate reconcilable Outstanding Receipts account.
  • Perform accounting actions with the required accountant access.
  • Confirm company, customer, currency and project before registering an advance.
03 / ADVANCES

Receive once. Recover across multiple invoices.

An advance is deliberately assigned to a customer and, when applicable, a sales-order/project context. Registration reclassifies the posted payment’s receivable credit into an advance liability.

STEP 1

Confirm payment

Create an incoming customer payment with a posted journal entry. Its receivable credit must be entirely unmatched for registration.

STEP 2

Assign & register

Click Assign Contract Advance, check the assignment, then Register Advance Liability. The assignment becomes immutable.

STEP 3

Apply to invoices

On a progress invoice, select the registered advance. Choose a percentage of eligible untaxed work or a fixed recovery amount.

STEP 4

Review availability

Posting transfers the recovered amount out of the advance liability. Recovered increases; remaining advance decreases.

Illustrative advance: 6,000 SAR
Recovered 2,000
Still available 4,000

Further invoices can use the remaining advance. Recovery cannot exceed the available advance or eligible untaxed work. Customer, company, currency and project must match the invoice.

05 / RETENTION RELEASE

Release makes held money collectible. It does not receive cash.

Use Contract Retention → Release Retention to select eligible items, review the amount, and post the release. A release cannot exceed the eligible remaining balance.

06 / BANK RECONCILIATION

“Remaining advance = 0” is not “Payment = Paid”.

Advance recovery settles the contract allocation. The payment’s Outstanding Receipts line still needs its bank counterpart.

Accounting eventDebitCredit
Incoming customer paymentOutstanding ReceiptsAccounts Receivable
Advance registrationAccounts ReceivableCustomer Advance Liability
Actual bank receiptBankOutstanding Receipts

From the Customer Contract Advances form

Create Bank Receipt → confirm actual receipt, choose date/journal/reference → create a draft → review and post → return to Reconcile Payment → select the eligible posted Outstanding Receipts credit → confirm the full match.

The Create Bank Receipt button hides once a linked receipt exists. After full bank settlement, Reconcile Payment hides and a Reconciled flag appears. Odoo computes the payment status; the module never forces Paid.

Manual reconciliation limits: company-currency payments only; equal full residual amounts; matching customer, company, account and currency; a posted two-line bank receipt. Draft, cancelled, partially matched or protected contract-transfer entries are not eligible. This wizard does not perform write-offs or FX settlement. If a receipt is missing, verify actual bank evidence before creating anything; do not duplicate a receipt merely to change payment status.

08 / CONTROL & AUDIT

Guardrails around the accounting, not cosmetic status changes.

The module uses posted journal entries and movement records to explain the balances.

  • Accountant checks and standard Odoo access controls apply to accounting actions.
  • Registered advances cannot be casually reassigned or edited; protected movement records are immutable.
  • Recovery is validated against the available advance; matching company/customer/currency/project is required.
  • Receipt creation checks for possible duplicates and creates a draft rather than silently posting cash.
  • Reconciliation is revalidated under locks and uses standard Odoo reconciliation without elevated access.
  • Corrections and release history remain connected to their accounting movements.
