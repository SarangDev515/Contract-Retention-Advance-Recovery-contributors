2. Configuration Setup

Configured under Accounting Settings → Contract Retention and Advances (Company-specific):

Contract Transfer Accounts

  • Enable contract accounting.
  • Reconcilable current-asset account for retention.
  • Reconcilable current-liability account for advances.
  • Miscellaneous journal for contract transfers.

Bank Settlement Accounts

  • Configure payment journal’s actual Bank account.
  • Separate reconcilable Outstanding Receipts account.
  • Perform accounting actions with required accountant access.
  • Confirm company, customer, currency and project.

3. Advance Recovery Workflow

Follow this diagrammatic step-by-step sequence to process advance recovery:

1 Confirm Payment: Create an incoming customer payment with a posted journal entry. Its receivable credit must be entirely unmatched for registration.
2 Assign & Register: Click Assign Contract Advance, check assignment, then Register Advance Liability. The assignment becomes immutable.
3 Apply to Invoices: On a progress invoice, select registered advance. Choose a percentage of eligible untaxed work or a fixed recovery amount.
4 Review Availability: Posting transfers recovered amount out of liability. Recovered increases; remaining advance decreases.
Example: advance of 6,000 SAR — Recovered 2,000 SAR, still available 4,000 SAR.
Further invoices can use remaining advance. Recovery cannot exceed available advance or eligible untaxed work. Customer, company, currency and project must match invoice.

4. Invoice Breakdown Matrix

Example parameters: untaxed work 10,000 SAR, tax 1,500 SAR, retention 10%, recovery 20%. Gross invoice: 11,500 SAR.

5. Retention Release Lifecycle

Lifecycle Progression:
Held (retention asset)  →  Released (customer receivable)  →  Collected later (receive and reconcile payment)

Posting a release debits Accounts Receivable and credits Retention account. Held balance reduces and amount becomes collectible in receivables. No bank cash is created by this action.

6. Bank Reconciliation

A Create Bank Receipt: Confirm actual receipt, choose date/journal/reference → create a draft.
B Review and Post: Review drafted entry → post entry → return to Reconcile Payment wizard.
C Full Reconciliation: Select eligible posted Outstanding Receipts credit → confirm full match.
Manual reconciliation limits: Company-currency payments only; equal full residual amounts; matching customer, company, account and currency; posted two-line bank receipt required.
