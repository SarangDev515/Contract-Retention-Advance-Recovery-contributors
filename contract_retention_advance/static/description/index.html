Contract Retention & Advance Recovery

Invoice the full value of the work. The app holds back the retention, recovers the customer's advance, and tells you exactly what the customer owes today — with every step posted as a normal, auditable journal entry.

One progress invoice, split three ways

Example: SAR 100,000 of work + 15% VAT = SAR 115,000 gross invoice, with 10% retention and 20% advance recovery.

Customer pays now 85,000 Advance 20,000 Held 10,000
85,000Immediate payable. Collected through the normal Pay button. 20,000Taken from the SAR 200,000 advance the customer already paid. 180,000 remains. 10,000Retention asset. Released later, in full or in parts, when you decide.

What problem does it solve?

Contracting and project businesses rarely invoice and collect in one clean step. The customer pays money up front, the contract keeps a percentage until handover, and every progress invoice has to reflect both. Doing this by hand in standard Odoo means manual journal entries and spreadsheets. This app does it from the invoice itself.

Customer advances

Receive a standalone payment, register it as an advance liability, and recover it automatically as a percentage or fixed amount on each progress invoice.

Retention

Hold a percentage of each invoice as a retention asset. Release it manually whenever the contract allows, fully or partially.

Clean receivables

Gross invoice and VAT never change. Deductions are posted as separate transfers and reconciled, so the customer's open balance always shows what is really collectible.

One report

See gross invoiced, VAT, retention held, released and remaining, plus advance received, recovered and remaining. Export it to Excel.

Both features are independent. Use retention only, advance recovery only, or both together as in the example above.

How it works, step by step

Ten steps from setup to report. Steps 1–4 are done once per customer or advance; steps 5–7 repeat for every progress period.

1

Configure the company

Accounting → Configuration → Settings → Contract Retention and Advances

Enable the feature and set your default retention and recovery percentages (the example uses 10% and 20%). Then map three things: a reconcilable retention account (Current Assets), a reconcilable advance account (Current Liabilities), and a Miscellaneous transfer journal. Settings are saved per company.

2

Prepare the customer

Check the customer's receivable account, company and currency. Then choose how you work:

  • ✓  Customer-only route: no project on the advance or the invoice.
  • ✓  Sales-order route: assign the advance to exactly the same order that generates the invoice.
3

Receive the customer's advance

Customers → Payments → New

Record the customer's payment (example: SAR 200,000) with a supported bank incoming payment method. Confirm it and check that the journal entry is posted and the customer receivable credit is still unreconciled.

Dr Outstanding Receipts / Bank     200,000
Cr Customer Receivable             200,000
A confirmed payment is not automatically a contract advance. It becomes one in the next step.
4

Register the advance liability

Payment → Assign Contract Advance → Register Advance Liability

Verify the customer, company, currency and project, then click Register Advance Liability. The unused payment credit is reclassified from receivable to a dedicated advance liability. No second receipt is created and no revenue is recognised.

Dr Customer Receivable             200,000
Cr Advance Liability               200,000
One advance per payment. Registration cannot be repeated and the assignment is locked afterwards. You can also start from Reporting → Customer Contract Advances.
5

Create the progress invoice

Customers → Invoices → New → Retention & Advance Recovery

Add the work and tax as usual. Switch on retention, enter the percentage (and the handover date if required), pick the matching advance, and choose a recovery percentage or a fixed amount. A live Calculation Summary shows the result before you post.

Work 100,000 + VAT 15,000   = Gross invoice 115,000
Retention  = 100,000 x 10%  =  10,000
Recovery   = 100,000 x 20%  =  20,000
Immediate payable           =  85,000
Deductions are calculated on net product lines after discounts. Section and note lines are ignored. Draft figures are a preview only. If no advance is selected, recovery is zero.
6

Post the invoice

Confirm the invoice. Odoo posts the standard entry (receivable 115,000, revenue 100,000, VAT 15,000). The app then creates two separate transfers and reconciles their receivable credits against the invoice.

Dr Retention Asset      10,000  /  Cr AR  10,000
Dr Advance Liability    20,000  /  Cr AR  20,000

Result before any cash: normal receivable 85,000, retention asset 10,000, advance liability left 180,000. Gross and VAT do not change.

7

Collect the immediate payable

Use the standard Pay / Register Payment button for the actual 85,000 receipt. Nothing special is needed.

The invoice can show "Partially Paid" simply because the deductions were reconciled, not because cash arrived. Amount Due is always the live residual. A paid invoice does not settle the separate retention.
8

Release retention

Reporting → Contract Retention → Release Retention, or from the customer

Select one company / customer / project / currency batch, review the date, journal and available retention, and enter the amount. Example: release SAR 5,000 of the SAR 10,000 held. The date cannot be earlier than the source invoice, and the amount must be positive and within the remaining balance.

Dr Customer Receivable      5,000
Cr Retention Asset          5,000
Release is always manual. It does not happen automatically at handover. Each source invoice gets its own move, and a completed request cannot be posted twice.
9

Collect the released retention

The released amount is now a normal receivable. Collect it with standard customer payment tools. Do not create a new invoice for it: the original invoice's gross and residual are not increased by a release.

10

Check balances and export

Reporting → Contract Retention & Advance Report

Choose the as-of date, company, customer, project (if used) and currency, then click Compute. Use the Invoices, Advances and Movements tabs to drill down, and Export Excel for a file with three sheets: Summary, Source transactions and Method.

The example, in numbers

What the report shows after the full example: one progress invoice, a partial retention release of 5,000, and 85,000 collected.

The 90,000 is not the live unpaid balance. Once 85,000 has been paid, only the 5,000 release receivable remains to be collected. Totals for different currencies are always kept separate.

Mistakes happen: corrections are built in

Nothing is deleted. Every correction is a new posted entry that offsets the old one, so the history stays complete.

Linked credit notes

Open the original invoice and click Credit Note. Enter the full or partial work being credited and post. Retention and advance recovery are corrected in proportion. This works before or after a retention release. Total credited work can never exceed the original work.

Reset to draft and repost

If the invoice has no customer payment, no retention release and no posted credit note, you can reset it, fix it, and confirm again. The old deductions are offset and a fresh set is posted. The example returns to retention 10,000 and advance remaining 180,000.

Example: partial release, then a full credit note

Original: work 100,000 + VAT 15,000, retention 10,000, recovery 20,000
Release of 5,000 already posted
Credit note gross:                     115,000
Dr AR  5,000  / Cr Retention          5,000   (unreleased part only)
Dr AR 20,000  / Cr Advance Liability 20,000   (advance restored)
Credit AR after deductions:            90,000

Remaining retention becomes zero and the remaining advance returns to 200,000. The released 5,000 stays as a customer credit in receivables. Match it with normal receivable reconciliation; it is not matched automatically. Cash refunds are separate payment actions.

Built-in safeguards

  • ✓  Recovery can never exceed the eligible work or the advance still available. Over-recovery is rejected.
  • ✓  Customer, company and currency must match across the advance, the invoice and the release.
  • ✓  Each payment can be registered as an advance only once, and its assignment is locked.
  • ✓  Customer, company and currency cannot be changed once contract history exists.
  • ✓  Cancellation, Reverse and Create Invoice, independent transfer reversal and manual unmatching are blocked on contract entries.
  • ✓  Standard Odoo lock dates and accounting restrictions still apply.
  • ✓  Original journal entries are never deleted. Corrections are always new offsetting entries.

Before you install

  • ✓  Requires Odoo 19 with the Accounting / Invoicing app.
  • ✓  Back up your database, restart Odoo, then install or upgrade contract_retention_advance. When upgrading from an earlier version, the upgrade creates the new posting-cycle fields.
  • ✓  Try credit-after-release and repeated reset/repost on a test database first.
  • ✓  Account codes are chosen by your company in settings; none are hard-coded.
  • ✓  Advance VAT and legal document treatment should be reviewed by your accountant. This app is not a tax, e-invoicing or ZATCA certification.
