Contract Retention & Advance Recovery

Odoo 19 · Accounting + Sales

Track money received before work, apply it across progress invoices, hold contractual retention, and release it when it becomes collectible.

1. Three balances, three different questions

This module separates contract accounting from cash settlement. Do not use one balance as proof of another.

BalanceWhat it tells youWhat reduces it
Remaining advanceHow much prepaid money is still available for future invoices.Posted advance recovery and its corrections.
Remaining retentionHow much contractual retention is still held.Posted release or retention corrections.
Outstanding ReceiptsWhether the incoming payment has been matched to bank receipt evidence.Bank reconciliation, not advance allocation.

2. Setup

In Accounting settings, open Contract Retention and Advances. Settings are company-specific.

Contract transfer accounts

  • Enable contract accounting.
  • Configure a reconcilable current-asset account for retention.
  • Configure a reconcilable current-liability account for advances.
  • Select a miscellaneous journal for contract transfers.
  • Set default retention/recovery percentages and the handover-date policy.

Bank settlement accounts

  • Configure the payment journal’s actual Bank account.
  • Use a separate reconcilable Outstanding Receipts account.
  • Perform accounting actions with the required accountant access.
  • Confirm company, customer, currency and project before registering an advance.

3. Advances: receive once, recover across multiple invoices

An advance is assigned to a customer and, when applicable, a sales-order/project context. Registration reclassifies the posted payment’s receivable credit into an advance liability.

StepWhat to do
1. Confirm paymentCreate an incoming customer payment with a posted journal entry. Its receivable credit must be entirely unmatched for registration.
2. Assign & registerClick Assign Contract Advance, check the assignment, then Register Advance Liability. The assignment becomes immutable.
3. Apply to invoicesOn a progress invoice, select the registered advance. Choose a percentage of eligible untaxed work or a fixed recovery amount.
4. Review availabilityPosting transfers the recovered amount out of the advance liability. Recovered increases; remaining advance decreases.
Example: advance of 6,000 SAR — Recovered 2,000 SAR, still available 4,000 SAR.
Further invoices can use the remaining advance. Recovery cannot exceed the available advance or eligible untaxed work. Customer, company, currency and project must match the invoice.

4. Invoice breakdown

Retention and percentage recovery are calculated on eligible untaxed invoice-line subtotals. The gross invoice and tax remain unchanged; contract transfers reclassify the receivable.

Example only: untaxed work 10,000 SAR, tax 1,500 SAR, retention 10%, recovery 20%. Gross invoice: 11,500 SAR.

ItemCalculationAmount
Retention deduction (held)10,000 × 10%1,000 SAR
Advance recovery10,000 × 20%2,000 SAR
Immediate payable before cash11,500 − 1,000 − 2,0008,500 SAR

Payable before cash is not the current unpaid residual. Actual cash receipts and reconciliations affect Odoo’s Amount Due separately.

5. Retention release

Use Contract Retention → Release Retention to select eligible items, review the amount, and post the release. A release cannot exceed the eligible remaining balance. Release makes held money collectible. It does not receive cash.

Held (retention asset) → Released (customer receivable) → Collected later (receive and reconcile a payment)

What posting a release changes: debit Accounts Receivable and credit the Retention account. The held balance reduces and the amount becomes collectible in receivables. No bank cash is created by this action.

graph LR A[Held: Retention Asset] -->|Release Retention Action| B[Released: Customer Receivable] B -->|Receive & Reconcile Payment| C[Collected / Paid]

6. Bank reconciliation

“Remaining advance = 0” is not “Payment = Paid”. Advance recovery settles the contract allocation. The payment’s Outstanding Receipts line still needs its bank counterpart.

Accounting eventDebitCredit
Incoming customer paymentOutstanding ReceiptsAccounts Receivable
Advance registrationAccounts ReceivableCustomer Advance Liability
Actual bank receiptBankOutstanding Receipts
From the Customer Contract Advances form:
Create Bank Receipt → confirm actual receipt, choose date/journal/reference → create a draft → review and post → return to Reconcile Payment → select the eligible posted Outstanding Receipts credit → confirm the full match.

The Create Bank Receipt button hides once a linked receipt exists. After full bank settlement, Reconcile Payment hides and a Reconciled flag appears. Odoo computes the payment status; the module never forces Paid.

Visual Flow: Bank Reconciliation Process

graph TD A[Create Bank Receipt] -->|Draft Receipt| B[Review & Post] B -->|Reconcile Payment Wizard| C[Match Outstanding Receipts Credit] C -->|Complete Match| D[Reconciled Flag Active]

Manual reconciliation limits: company-currency payments only; equal full residual amounts; matching customer, company, account and currency; a posted two-line bank receipt. Draft, cancelled, partially matched or protected contract-transfer entries are not eligible. This wizard does not perform write-offs or FX settlement. If a receipt is missing, verify actual bank evidence before creating anything; do not duplicate a receipt merely to change payment status.

7. Reporting

Open Contract Retention & Advance Report. Use Date From, Date To, Company, Customer and Project; click Get Report to generate the table. The report shows gross invoiced, net held, released, advance received, recovered and remaining advance, with Invoices, Advances and Movements drill-downs.

  • Report scope: posted transactions within the inclusive date range. Opening balances before Date From are excluded; remaining figures are net period amounts, not closing balances.
  • Editing filters: existing rows remain visible until Get Report is clicked again.
  • Print: opens the browser print dialog through a hidden frame, without navigating to a new tab. The template specifies A4 landscape; browser settings can override orientation.
  • PDF: uses the same landscape template and generated report rows. A working Odoo PDF renderer is required.
  • Print/PDF subtotals: bold, separated by company and transaction currency. No mixed-currency grand total.
  • Excel: Summary, Source transactions and Method sheets; filter-details rows are omitted and subtotals are bold. Export calculations use the selected filters at export time.

8. Control & audit

The module uses posted journal entries and movement records to explain the balances.

  • Accountant checks and standard Odoo access controls apply to accounting actions.
  • Registered advances cannot be casually reassigned or edited; protected movement records are immutable.
  • Recovery is validated against the available advance; matching company/customer/currency/project is required.
  • Receipt creation checks for possible duplicates and creates a draft rather than silently posting cash.
  • Reconciliation is revalidated under locks and uses standard Odoo reconciliation without elevated access.
  • Corrections and release history remain connected to their accounting movements.
