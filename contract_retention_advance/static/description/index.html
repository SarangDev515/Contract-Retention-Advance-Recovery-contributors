Contract Retention & Advance Recovery

Odoo 19 · Accounting + Sales

Track money received before work, apply it across progress invoices, hold contractual retention, and release it when collectible.

1. Three Balances, Three Different Questions

This module separates contract accounting from cash settlement. Hover over each card to view details:

Remaining Advance

How much prepaid money is still available for future progress invoices.

Reduced by: Posted advance recovery & corrections.

Remaining Retention

How much contractual retention is currently held back.

Reduced by: Posted release or retention corrections.

Outstanding Receipts

Whether incoming customer payment is matched to bank receipt evidence.

Reduced by: Bank reconciliation.

2. Configuration Setup

Configured under Accounting Settings → Contract Retention and Advances (Company-specific):

Contract Transfer Accounts

  • Enable contract accounting.
  • Reconcilable current-asset account for retention.
  • Reconcilable current-liability account for advances.
  • Miscellaneous journal for contract transfers.

Bank Settlement Accounts

  • Configure payment journal’s actual Bank account.
  • Separate reconcilable Outstanding Receipts account.
  • Confirm company, customer, currency and project.

3. Advance Workflow: Receive Once, Recover Across Invoices

1
Confirm Payment: Create incoming customer payment with a posted journal entry (receivable credit unmatched).
2
Assign & Register: Click Assign Contract Advance and register advance liability (assignment becomes immutable).
3
Apply to Invoices: On a progress invoice, select registered advance & choose recovery percentage or fixed amount.
4
Review Availability: Posting transfers recovered amounts out of liability; remaining advance decreases.
Example Breakdown: Advance of 6,000 SAR — Recovered 2,000 SAR, remaining available 4,000 SAR. Recovery cannot exceed available advance or eligible untaxed work.

4. Invoice Breakdown Example

Untaxed work: 10,000 SAR | Tax: 1,500 SAR | Retention: 10% | Recovery: 20% | Gross Invoice: 11,500 SAR

Item Calculation Amount
Retention Deduction (Held) 10,000 × 10% 1,000 SAR
Advance Recovery 10,000 × 20% 2,000 SAR
Immediate Payable Before Cash 11,500 − 1,000 − 2,000 8,500 SAR

5. Retention Release Lifecycle

State Flow: Held (Retention Asset) → Released (Customer Receivable) → Collected Later (Receive & Reconcile Payment)

Posting a release debits Accounts Receivable and credits the Retention account. This makes held money collectible in receivables without generating bank cash directly.

6. Bank Reconciliation Process

A
Create Bank Receipt: Confirm actual receipt, choose date/journal/reference to create draft receipt.
B
Review & Post: Validate receipt entries and return to the Reconcile Payment wizard.
C
Match Credit: Select eligible posted Outstanding Receipts credit and confirm full match.
Manual Reconciliation Limits: Company-currency payments only; equal full residual amounts; matching customer, company, account, and currency; posted two-line bank receipt required.
